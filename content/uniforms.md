## Compulsory uniform

<ul class="kit-rules">
  <li>A <strong>MENC A-line netball dress</strong>.</li>
  <li>In line with FDNA Uniform Policy and Procedures, the entire team must be identical unless they are a gender-mixed team.</li>
  <li><strong>Sports briefs</strong> in black or matching our dress.</li>
  <li><strong>Bike shorts</strong> must not be visible beneath the dress or shorts.</li>
  <li>Players may choose to wear full-length leggings or tracksuit pants beneath their dress or shorts, in black or a colour matching the uniform.</li>
</ul>

<div class="measure-card">
  <h3>How to measure</h3>
  <p>Every size chart on this page is in centimetres, and they all use the same measurement.</p>
  <ol class="measure-steps">
    <li>Choose a garment that already fits you well. If you are ordering a hoodie, measure a hoodie, not a jacket.</li>
    <li>Lay the garment flat on a surface, with no wrinkles in it.</li>
    <li>Measure across the chest, from underarm to underarm. This is the half chest measurement.</li>
    <li>Compare your measurement with the chart under the item you are ordering and pick the closest match.</li>
  </ol>
  <p class="measure-note">The right size depends on the cut of the garment, so use the chart for the item you are ordering. If you fall between two sizes, take the next size up for a more comfortable fit.</p>
</div>
