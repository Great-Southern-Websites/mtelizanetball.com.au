---

Netball runs alongside school for most of our players. Every junior age group, from NetSetGO through to 17&U, trains on a weekday afternoon once the school day finishes, then plays on Saturday at Jubilee Park. Teams train at the club courts in Howard Parker Reserve and at Mount Eliza Secondary College, so getting to a session rarely means a long trip.

## What a season asks of a player

Training develops netball skills, and it develops cohesion as a team. The habits that come with it are the ones school asks for too: turning up each week, letting your coach know in advance when you cannot make a session, and holding your position while six other players hold theirs. Juniors learn to take direction from a coach, sort out a problem with a teammate, and keep playing when a game is not going their way.

## Somewhere to belong outside the classroom

A team gives a child a second group to belong to, with its own coach, its own routine and people who notice when they are not there. For players who find the school week hard going, an hour of running at training and a game on Saturday morning can be the part of the week they look forward to.

## Room for every level

MENC fields over 20 teams each winter, from NetSetGO beginners to Open division, and we have welcomed boys since the early 2000s. Players chasing a representative pathway and players who just want a run both have a side to play in.
